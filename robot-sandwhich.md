# Having a Robot make a Sandwich
## Assumptions 
* Bread is out of the bread bag on a plate on the counter
* Knife is on the counter Left of the plate
* The peanut butter and jelly are right side up unscrewed by the plate
* the robots hands are empty 
### Steps
1. Pick up the knife with your left hand
2. pick up the peanut butter jar with your right hand
3. insert the knife into the peanut butter jar
4. scoop the peanut butter with the knife and then remove the knife from the jar
5. Put the peanut butter jar down with your right hand
6. use your right hand to pick up a piece of bread
7. use the knife to spread the peanut butter on the slice of bread
8. put the bread slice back down from your right hand
9. Pick up the jelly jar with your right hand
10. insert the knife into the jelly jar
11. scoop out the jelly with the knife and then remove the knife from jar
12. put the jar back down with your right hand
13. pick up the piece of bread that does not have peanut butter on it
14. Use the knife to spread the jelly on the slice of bread
15. put the knife on the counter using your left hand
16. take your left hand and pick up the bread slice with jelly on it
17. take your right hand and pick up the bread slice with peanut butter on it
18. with the covered sides facing each other, put the 2 pieces of bread together
    ### Done When
 You are Done when the 2 pieces of bread are together with the peanut butter and jelly facing each other.
